# Privacy Policy — Football Duel

*Last updated: 23 August 2026*

Football Duel ("the app", "we", "us") is a football (soccer) trivia game. This
policy explains what data the app handles and why. We keep it minimal on
purpose: the app does not require registration, does not ask for your legal
name, email, or other contact details, and does not show ads. It automatically
creates a technical guest account when multiplayer features need one.

## Information we handle

**Anonymous identifier.** When you open the app, we create an anonymous session
with our backend provider (Supabase) so the game can work (for example, to run
multiplayer rooms). This identifier is not linked to your real identity.

**Display name (multiplayer only).** If you create or join a multiplayer room,
the display name you type is shared with the other player in that room and stored
with the room so the game can show who is who. Please do not enter personal
information as a display name.

**Gameplay data.** Your in-game answers and round results are processed on our
backend to run the game (validate answers, decide the winner, keep score during
a match).

**On-device storage.** Your best scores and some reference data (team lists) are
stored locally on your device to make the app fast and to remember your high
scores. This data stays on your device and is not tied to your identity.

## What we do NOT do

- We do **not** collect your name, email address, phone number, or precise
location.
- We do **not** use third-party advertising or advertising identifiers.
- We do **not** track you across other apps or websites.
- We do **not** sell or share your data with third parties for marketing.



## Service providers

We use **Supabase** (database, anonymous authentication, and realtime) to run
the app's backend. Data you send while playing (anonymous id, display name in a
room, gameplay events) is processed on Supabase's infrastructure on our behalf.

## Data retention

Multiplayer room, match, answer, and guest-profile records are retained in our
backend until you delete your guest account or we remove old operational data.
We do not currently promise a fixed automatic deletion period. On-device data
(high scores, cached team lists) remains until you delete the app, clear its
data, or use the in-app deletion control.

## Children's privacy

The app is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly request
personal contact information from children. It requires no registration or
personal contact details.

## Your choices

The app creates an automatically generated guest account for multiplayer. You
can delete that guest account and its associated game-profile data from
**Settings & Privacy → Delete Account**. You can remove locally stored data by
using that control or deleting the app.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The "Last updated" date at the top
reflects the latest version.
